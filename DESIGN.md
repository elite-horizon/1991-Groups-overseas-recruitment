## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an atmosphere of executive authority, absolute regulatory compliance, and life-changing international opportunity. Geared toward two core audiences—discerning corporate clients seeking enterprise overseas staffing and ambitious international job candidates seeking verified placements—the visual tone replaces antiquated bureaucratic agency tropes with institutional prestige and streamlined modernity.

The visual direction follows **Corporate / Modern** anchored by editorial precision:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, dependable, transparent, and globally aspirational.
- **Visual Weight:** Lightweight, expansive whitespace balanced by high-density tabular and metric information modules.
- **Key Motifs:** Precision lines, structured data panels, verified credential badging, and decisive action pathways (e.g., instant WhatsApp consultations and active requisition indexing).

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast nautical executive tones paired with warm kinetic accents to convey international maritime scale and institutional integrity.

- **Primary (`#0A2540` - Deep Oceanic Navy):** Forms the foundational identity. Governs primary navigation, hero text headers, primary call-to-actions, and structural surface anchors. Communicates stability, global governance, and legal authority.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B` - Amber Gold):** The beacon of opportunity and transition. Applied sparingly to critical micro-interactions, active status badges, highlight metrics, and high-conversion conversion triggers (such as overseas inquiry points).
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7` - Voyage Cyan):** Delivers supportive utility for process progress indicators, dynamic flight/relocation maps, and secondary interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate):** Regulates descriptive typography, structural border dividers, subtle table alternating bands, and non-active state elements.
- **Canvas & Surface Tiering:** Crisp background whites (`#FFFFFF`) with cool slate foundation washes (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`) ensuring a lightweight, breathable UI without heavy visual clutter.

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its crisp geometric construction, modern editorial feel, and high legibility across tabular datasets and promotional layouts.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with tighter negative letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700`, `800`) to project institutional firmness and executive credibility.
- **Body:** Calibrated at `15px` (`body-md`) and `18px` (`body-lg`) to balance swift scanning of statutory overseas criteria, job descriptions, and partnership briefs.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Configured with elevated weights (`600`, `700`) and slight positive letter-spacing (`0.05em` on `label-sm`) for verification stamps, country codes, license numbers, and category tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model enforces a **12-column responsive fluid grid** with an architectural max-width container of `1280px` for standard editorial layouts and an ultra-wide `1440px` for metric dashboards.

- **Desktop (>=1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, and `4rem` (`64px`) page boundary margins. Allows side-by-side split heroes, asymmetric data grids, and structured job filter navigation.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Job category matrices consolidate from 4 columns to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins. Cards, application funnels, and metric statistics transition to single-column stacking with horizontal swipe patterns for process steps.
- **Rhythm:** Multiples of `8px` guide all structural containers. Vertical section separations scale from `4rem` (`64px`) on mobile to `7.5rem` (`120px`) on desktop to maintain an uncluttered corporate presence.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain a crisp corporate presence, depth is generated primarily through **low-contrast micro-borders** complemented by subtle, multi-stage **ambient shadows**. Elements appear grounded rather than floating.

- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** Background layers (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`). No shadow. Borderless or separated by hairline slate rules (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Surface Elevation):** Static job cards, service summaries, and metric boards. Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Process Steps):** Active states on category links, process cards, and pinned filter toolbars. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.03)`. Micro-border transitions to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Conversion Hubs):** Floating WhatsApp inquiry hubs, recruitment filter drawers, and modal credential dossiers. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (`1`)** shape language, conveying corporate stability, precision, and architectural order rather than casual softness.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Checkboxes, status dots, tabular tags, and verified indicator chips.
- **Card & Input Radius (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / `8px`):** Requisition cards, global category tiles, form fields, and modal containers.
- **Pill Exception (`9999px`):** Strictly reserved for dynamic country badge indicators (e.g., "🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia", "🇦🇪 UAE"), quick-action filter pills, and instant contact CTA buttons to create clear interactive distinction.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Executive Navy):** Background `#0A2540`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.5rem`. Hover triggers a solid shift to `#1E3A5F` with subtle vertical expansion (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Accent (Opportunity Gold / WhatsApp Action):** Background `#D97706`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.5rem`. Dedicated to high-value recruitment conversion paths and emergency corporate talent requests.
- **WhatsApp Direct Connect:** Background `#25D366` or Navy with green indicator accents, paired with bold typography and direct routing icons.
- **Outline / Secondary:** Surface transparent, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0A2540`. Hover transitions surface to `#F8FAFC`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Process & Compliance Badges:** Monochromatic slate or soft gold backgrounds (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`), text `#B45309`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`. Label set in uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Location & Vacancy Chips:** Pill-shaped, background `#F1F5F9`, text `#0A2540`, icon leading with country flag or industry icon.

### Cards & Grids
- **Metric Highlight Cards:** Elevated stats featuring large display numbers in `#0A2540` or `#D97706`, accompanied by an anchored subtitle and regulatory audit accreditation badge.
- **Job Category Grid Tiles:** Structured with a minimalist slate border (`#E2E8F0`), leading industry icon in Deep Navy, title, live opening count badge, and a directional arrow indicator visible on hover.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, internal padding `0.75rem 1rem`, typography `body-md`. Focus state implements a clean `2px` focus ring in `#0A2540` with an offset of `1px`.

### Global Process Timeline
- Step-by-step visa processing and deployment tracker modules featuring numbered geometric markers (`#0A2540`), connecting rules in `#E2E8F0`, and status tags designating legal stages (e.g., "Medical Clearance", "Embassy Attestation", "Flight Dispatch").
